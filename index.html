Research Interests

My research focuses on algorithm–system co-design for efficient and scalable AI infrastructure. I explore this goal from three complementary directions:

  • Efficient inference algorithmsReducing computational and memory costs through training-free sparsification and quantization, with designs that translate algorithmic savings into practical speedups.
    Related past work: PAROAttention.
  • Efficient inference systemsExploiting workload characteristics to improve resource utilization through load balancing, adaptive parallelism, and KV-cache scheduling, from visual generation to agentic LLM serving.
    Related past work: db-SP and DualPD.
  • Efficient post-training algorithmsDeveloping methods that encourage concise reasoning and effective collaboration between large and small models, reducing inference costs while maintaining or improving task performance.
    Related past work: QLPO and OARS.
